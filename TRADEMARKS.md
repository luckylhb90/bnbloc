# Trademark policy

The bnbloc **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
The bnbloc **name and logo** are not: the license covers copyright, and its
section 6 explicitly grants no rights to use the project's trade names or
marks. This policy explains what you can do with them.

The goal is simple: anyone who sees "bnbloc" should be able to trust that it
is the project's software or a faithful copy of it.

## Fine without asking

- Refer to the project truthfully: "runs on bnbloc", "a component for
  bnbloc", "compatible with bnbloc", "we contribute to bnbloc".
- Distribute **unmodified** official source or release binaries under the
  bnbloc name.
- Run bnbloc for your own organization, including inside a company, and call
  it bnbloc internally.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and reviews.
- Name a component, plugin or integration "<something> for bnbloc", as long
  as it is clear you made it, not the bnbloc project.

## Needs written permission

- Distributing a **modified** version (a fork, a rebuilt binary with changed
  code, a repackaged distribution) under the bnbloc name or logo. Forks are
  welcome; give yours its own name and say it is "based on bnbloc".
- Offering bnbloc **as a hosted or managed service** under the bnbloc name
  (for example "bnbloc Cloud" or "Managed bnbloc"). You may host the software
  under your own brand and say it is "powered by bnbloc".
- Using the name or logo in a product name, company name, domain name or
  social media handle.
- Merchandise, or anything suggesting the project endorses you.

## Logo

Use the logo only unmodified and only to refer to the project. Do not alter
its colors or proportions, and do not combine it with your own marks.

## Asking

Open an issue titled "Trademark request" describing the intended use. We say
yes to most good-faith requests.
