# Licensing: CLA and trademark policy

Status: landed 2026-10-10.

## Intent

The code stays Apache-2.0 (v0.1.0 was released under it). Two additions keep
the project's commercial options open without changing the license:

- **CLA** (`CLA.md`, based on the Apache ICLA): contributors grant the
  maintainers a license to distribute contributions under any terms, so a
  future version can change license (for example to protect a hosted
  offering) without tracking down every contributor. Released versions stay
  under their release's license. Accepted per pull request through the
  `contributor-assistant` action (`.github/workflows/cla.yml`); signatures
  live on the `cla-signatures` branch.
- **Trademark policy** (`TRADEMARKS.md`): Apache-2.0 section 6 grants no
  trademark rights. The name and logo stay reserved for the official
  software: unmodified redistribution and truthful references are free;
  modified distributions and hosted services under the bnbloc name need
  permission. Forks are welcome under their own name.

README (both languages), CONTRIBUTING and NOTICE point to both documents.

## Not legal advice

These documents follow common open-core practice. Have a lawyer review them
before relying on them commercially, and before the first outside
contribution is merged.
